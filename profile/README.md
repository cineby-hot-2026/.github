# Cineby

Catalogue of films and TV series at [cineby.hot](https://cineby.hot/).

- Films and series with filters by genre, year and country
- Ratings shown together with the source they come from
- Watchlist, folders, ratings and viewing history for signed-in visitors
- Sign in with a one-time email link or with Google, no payment details

Cineby is operated by CineBy Limited Hanoi, Vietnam. This organisation is the GitHub home of the cineby.hot team.
